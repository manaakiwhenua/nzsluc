Transportation

3.6.0skos:Concept

https://nzlum.landcareresearch.co.nz/landuse/concept/transport

Definition

Land allocated to infrastructure used for the transportation of goods or people.

Usage note

Zoning of land for roading or rail purposes that has not yet been constructed should be captured as attributes.

Change notes

Redefinition of class 3.6.0 to exclude navigation and communication infrastructure, which is now captured under 3.5.8. Renamed from 'Transport and communication'.
