Utilities

3.5.0skos:Concept

https://nzlum.landcareresearch.co.nz/landuse/concept/utilities

Definition

Land allocated to providing electricity, gas, or water; or for navigation and communication infrastructure.

Change notes

Class 3.5.0 redefined at v0.4.0 to include naviation and communciation infrastructure, which was formerly captured under class 3.6.0.
