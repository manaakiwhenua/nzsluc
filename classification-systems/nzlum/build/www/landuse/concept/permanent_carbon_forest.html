Permanent carbon forest

2.1.5skos:Concept

https://nzlum.landcareresearch.co.nz/landuse/concept/permanent_carbon_forest

This concept is deprecated: it is not part of the current NZLUM version.

Definition

An area planted with indigenous or exotic trees for the purpose of gaining carbon credits (carbon farming).

History note

Last included in NZLUM 0.3.0; not carried into NZLUM 0.4.0.

Change note

Retired at NZLUM v0.3 and replaced with consideration of carbon sequestration and carbon credits in the definitions of exotic and indigenous plantation forestry.
