Transport and communication

3.6.0skos:Concept

https://nzlum.landcareresearch.co.nz/landuse/concept/transport_communication

This concept is deprecated: it is not part of the current NZLUM version.

Definition

Land allocated to infrastructure used for the transportation of goods or people, or navigation and communication equipment.

Usage note

Zoning of land for roading or rail purposes that has not yet been constructed should be captured as attributes.

History note

Last included in NZLUM 0.3.0; not carried into NZLUM 0.4.0.
